Where Support Goes

Nothing here is taken on trust.

  • Every project is public

    Dated, budgeted and located on the Development Tracker.

  • Funding is labelled

    Foundation-funded, Government (NG-CDF) or Partner-funded, on every project.

  • Claims are checked on site

    A site visit comes before any status is published.

Questions

Straight answers before you get involved.

Can I give money online?

Not yet. Online giving is not open. If you would like to give, tell us using the form above and the team will get in touch with how.

Where does support go?

To projects on the Development Tracker, each labelled with its funding source and checked on site before its status is published.

Can I help if I live outside Embakasi South?

Yes. Partners and the diaspora can follow the work and back it from anywhere, because the record is open to everyone. Volunteering on the ground is easiest for residents.

How do I know the work is real?

Every project and pledge is public, dated and open to check. See how verification works.
