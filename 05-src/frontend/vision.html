Long-term plan

Priorities

What guides the long-term plan, beyond any single term in office, framed against the same development goals the international community already tracks, because accountability shouldn't need translation.

Priority 01

Healthcare

Every ward with reliable access to a functioning clinic: not just outreach camps, but permanent care residents can count on.

UN SDG 3 Good Health and Well-Being

Embakasi South's healthcare priority, permanent clinics rather than outreach camps alone, sits inside this same global goal.

How we'll know it's working

[Measurable target to be set with Erick, e.g. how many wards have a permanent clinic]

See the Health promises on the Scorecard
Priority 02

Education

Schools, bursaries, and digital literacy that give every child in Embakasi South a real shot, not just a nearby classroom.

UN SDG 4 Quality Education

Schools, bursaries, and digital literacy for every child here map directly onto this target.

How we'll know it's working

[Measurable target to be set with Erick, e.g. ECDE classrooms built and teachers on permanent terms]

See the Education promises on the Scorecard
Priority 03

Housing & Infrastructure

Upgraded settlements, roads that hold up through a rainy season, and drainage that doesn't flood the same streets every year.

UN SDG 9 & 11 Infrastructure & Sustainable Communities

Roads, drainage, and upgraded settlements: the same standard the UN sets for cities worldwide, applied ward by ward.

How we'll know it's working

[Measurable target to be set with Erick, e.g. roads upgraded and street lights installed]

See the Infrastructure promises on the Scorecard
Priority 04

Youth & Employment

Jobs and enterprise support that don't require leaving the constituency to find real opportunity.

UN SDG 8 Decent Work and Economic Growth

Jobs and enterprise support that keep opportunity inside the constituency, not just passing through it.

How we'll know it's working

[Measurable target to be set with Erick, e.g. people trained or supported through the empowerment programmes]

The Horizon

Three phases, from a verified baseline to a record built to outlast any one term.

  1. Real photo needed: a site visit in progress
    Phase 1 2026 to 2027

    Verified Baseline

    Every ward's current infrastructure, health, education, and water access status documented and published before the 2027 election.

  2. Real photo needed: a completed project
    Phase 2 2027 to 2032

    Delivery Under Watch

    Every project promised during the term tracked to completion, or explained, in public, in real time.

  3. Real photo needed: the tracker in use
    Phase 3 2032 and beyond

    A Standing Record

    Embakasi South's permanent, independent development record, open to residents, researchers, and partners alike, regardless of who holds the seat.
