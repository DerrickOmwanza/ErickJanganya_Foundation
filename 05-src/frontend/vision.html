Vision

The Embakasi South we're building toward — where the constituency should be in five to ten years, beyond the priorities of a single term.

Hero photo — Embakasi South, a wide shot

Priorities

What guides the long-term plan, beyond any single term in office.

Priority photo — Healthcare

Healthcare

Every ward with reliable access to a functioning clinic — not just outreach camps, but permanent care residents can count on.

Priority photo — Education

Education

Schools, bursaries, and digital literacy that give every child in Embakasi South a real shot, not just a nearby classroom.

Priority photo — Housing & Infrastructure

Housing & Infrastructure

Upgraded settlements, roads that hold up through a rainy season, and drainage that doesn't flood the same streets every year.

Priority photo — Youth & Employment

Youth & Employment

Jobs and enterprise support that don't require leaving the constituency to find real opportunity.

This platform doesn't end at an election. It evolves — from candidate site, to campaign platform, to MP accountability record, to a standing account of Embakasi South's progress, whatever the outcome in 2027.
