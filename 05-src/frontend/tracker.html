    How to Read This Tracker

    Every project carries a status, a funding source and a set of numbers. This is what each one means.

    Status

    • Planned

      Approved or announced, but no work has started on site yet.

    • Ongoing

      Work is under way. The progress figure is how far along it was when last checked on site.

    • Completed

      The work is finished and has been checked on site by the team.

    Funding source

    • Foundation-funded

      Paid for by the Erick Janganya Foundation, from the donations it has received.

    • Government (NG-CDF)

      Paid for from public money, such as the National Government Constituency Development Fund. The Foundation documents it; it did not fund it.

    • Partner-funded

      Paid for by a partner organisation or donor working alongside the Foundation.

    Progress
    The share of the project's work that is finished, as last verified on site.
    Budget
    The amount set aside for the whole project, not the money spent so far.
    Updated
    When the record last changed, so you can see how fresh the information is.

    Every record is checked before it is published. A team member visits the site, the status is set from what is actually there, and the budget and funding source are attached.
