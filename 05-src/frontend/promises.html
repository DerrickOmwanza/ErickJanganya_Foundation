How We Score

Three marks, one standard: nothing is marked on a claim alone.

01

The three marks

  1. Photo: a delivered project
    Kept 0

    The promise has been delivered.

    What it takesThe work is finished and checked on site, with the proof attached.

  2. Photo: work under way
    In progress 0

    Work towards the promise has started.

    What it takesA project is under way on the tracker, and its progress has been verified.

  3. Photo: a site before work begins
    Not started 0

    Nothing has been delivered yet.

    What it takesThe pledge is on the record, waiting for work to begin. Nothing is rounded up.
