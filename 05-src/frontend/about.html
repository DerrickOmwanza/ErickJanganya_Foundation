About Erick Janganya

I am Erick Janganya, an aspiring Member of Parliament for Embakasi South, based in Nairobi, and I'm running to represent Embakasi South in the 2027 general election because leadership should be something you can check, not something you are asked to take on trust.

I believe every project needs a date and a budget, every promise needs a record, and every resident, whether in Embakasi South, across Kenya or anywhere in the world, deserves the same honest account of what was delivered. Embakasi South is where that standard starts. Kenya and the wider world are where it should go.

Before you give anyone else the chance to lead you, ask whether they will let you check their work. I will, and I will work for the people, whatever it takes.

Why I'm Running

The record has to be honest first

Three things this campaign comes down to.

Photo: an unfinished or stalled project site

A pattern, not a lack of ideas

A borehole promised at a rally, never dug. A road resurfaced only where the cameras could see it. A youth fund announced, never disbursed. The problem was never short on ideas. It was that nobody checked whether they became real.

Photo: Erick reviewing tracker records on site

The same standard, applied to me

That's the gap the foundation exists to close: every project and promise made to this constituency, dated and open to verify, whether or not I made it. This campaign is the next step of that work, not a departure from it.

Photo: Erick speaking directly with residents

What I'm asking for

Not another name promising it'll be different this time. A representative willing to be checked the same way everyone else already is on this site. That's the only thing I'm asking the chance to be.

Photo: a resident reviewing a project record

Transparency

Every project and promise carries a date, a budget line, and a status, with nothing claimed without proof attached.

Photo: a completed project compared to what was promised

Accountability

Progress is measured against what was actually promised, not against what's convenient to say afterward.

Photo: a ward visit, any of the five

Presence

Imara Daima, Kwa Njenga, Kwa Reuben, Pipeline, and Kware: tracked individually, not blurred into one average.

Photo: a returning site visit, same project over time

Continuity

The record doesn't reset on election day. Whatever happens in 2027, the tracker keeps running.

What Guides This

Four things that don't move: transparency, accountability, presence, and continuity.
