An Open Record

Why I'm Running

The record has to be honest first

I didn't set out to be a politician. I set out to fix a pattern I kept seeing repeat itself across Embakasi South — a borehole promised at a rally and never dug, a road resurfaced only where the cameras could see it, a youth fund announced with fanfare and never actually disbursed. The pattern wasn't a lack of good ideas. It was a lack of anyone checking whether the ideas ever became real.

That's the gap the Erick Janganya Foundation exists to close — tracking every project and every promise made to this constituency, dated and open for any resident to verify, whether or not I'm the one who made it. Running for this seat is the next step of that same work, not a departure from it.

Embakasi South doesn't need another name on a ballot promising things will be different this time. It needs a representative willing to be checked the same way everyone else already is on this site — and that's exactly what I'm asking for the chance to be.

“The pattern wasn't a lack of good ideas. It was a lack of anyone checking whether the ideas ever became real.”
Erick Janganya
