Core Principles

Four things every project on this site has to answer to before it's marked verified.

01 Accountability

Accountability

Every claim traces back to a project record anyone can check. Nothing stands on reputation alone.

See it on the tracker
02 Transparency

Transparency

Budgets, funding sources and progress are published as work happens, not summarised after the fact.

See funding sources
03 Sustainable Development

Sustainable Development

Projects are chosen to outlast a single term, built to serve Embakasi South well beyond one election cycle.

Read the vision
04 Integrity

Integrity

Credit is only claimed for work the foundation actually delivered. Never overstated, never borrowed.

Read Erick's story

How Verification Works

What separates a claim from a checked fact on this site.

Process photo — site visit

Step 1 — Site visit logged

A team member visits in person and photographs the project as it stands, dated on arrival.

Process photo — status check

Step 2 — Status set

Marked Planned, Ongoing, or Completed based on what's actually there — not what was announced.

Process photo — budget records

Step 3 — Budget & funding source attached

Every project is labelled honestly: Foundation-funded, Government (NG-CDF), or Partner-funded.

Process photo — public record

Step 4 — Public record updated

The tracker and, where linked, the Promise Scorecard both update — visible to anyone, immediately.
